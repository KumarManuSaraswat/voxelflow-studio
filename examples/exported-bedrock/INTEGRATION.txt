VoxelFlow Studio — voxelflow:atlas_robot

This is a resource asset package, not a complete playable mob.
Merge these files into a Bedrock resource pack, or enable this resource pack with a behavior pack defining the same identifier.
Configure your behavior entity's collision box, health, movement, spawn rules, and attack behavior.
The controller uses query.modified_move_speed and query.is_delayed_attacking. A behavior with a delayed attack must drive the latter; otherwise the punch remains previewable in Blockbench only.
Cyan eye pixels are bright colors, not an emissive material.
Numeric, linear/step keyframes and per-face cube UVs are supported.
Validate in your Minecraft Bedrock version. Runtime compatibility is NOT verified.
