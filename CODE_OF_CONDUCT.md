# Community Code of Conduct

VoxelFlow Studio welcomes participants of every background and skill level. These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces officially managed by the project.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and specific. Critique ideas and work without attacking people. Make room for beginners, accept constructive feedback, credit other people's contributions, and respect privacy and boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, impersonation, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Do not publish someone's private information or continue contact after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's report-content controls for abuse when appropriate. For a project moderation concern, contact a maintainer through a contact method they explicitly make public. If no private channel is available, open an issue requesting a private moderation contact without posting personal details or the report itself. Do not use public issues to disclose sensitive allegations. If the concern involves the only maintainer, GitHub's reporting route remains available independently of this project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, request a behavior change, close a discussion, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation depending on severity and repetition. They should consider context, explain decisions where practical, and protect reporters' privacy. A person directly involved in a report should not decide its outcome when another uninvolved maintainer is available.

An affected participant may request reconsideration through a private channel agreed with an uninvolved maintainer. During the single-maintainer stage, an independent project appeal may not be available; GitHub's own reporting and appeal processes remain separate.
